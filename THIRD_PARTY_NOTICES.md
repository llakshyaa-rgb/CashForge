# Third-party inspiration and attribution

CashForge is an independent implementation. No source files, distinctive text, assets, or dependency stacks have been transplanted from the repositories below. The MIT-licensed projects are credited for the general workflow ideas that informed product planning. CashForge's implementation, data model, local-first runtime, interface, and tests are its own.

- [multi-agent-market-research](https://github.com/wasifrepo/multi-agent-market-research) — MIT License. Reviewed its staged research orchestration, source discovery, trend and review analysis, competitor research, feasibility scoring, opportunity design, go-to-market planning, skeptic review, and citation-backed reporting. CashForge independently implements a smaller local-first subset using its own standard-library pipeline. The upstream project retains its copyright and licence.
- [trend-opportunity-radar](https://github.com/datou202307-design/trend-opportunity-radar) — MIT License. Reviewed its bounded collection, evidence provenance, counter-signal review, semantic clustering audits, report consistency, and longitudinal monitoring contracts. CashForge independently implements selected evidence-boundary, counter-signal, trend-history, and export concepts. Platform-specific adapters, audited semantic review, and external task scheduling are not claimed as implemented. The upstream project retains its copyright and licence.
- [venturemind-ai](https://github.com/mkhanna-stack/venturemind-ai) — no licence declared in the repository metadata when reviewed on 2026-11-02. Its public architecture and code were inspected only to understand its high-level processing flow: text cleaning, embedding-based grouping, vector retrieval, competitor discovery, gap hypotheses, opportunity scoring, idea generation, and time-series trend analysis. No source code, assets, distinctive text, model files, or implementation details were copied. CashForge independently uses standard-library TF-IDF-style weighting, conservative centroid grouping, its own evidence schema, transparent score calculations, local trend aggregation, and original validation/idea templates. These are independently written general-purpose techniques, not a port of VentureMind.
